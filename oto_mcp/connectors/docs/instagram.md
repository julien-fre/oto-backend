## prerequisite — connect your instagram account

your Instagram account is connected through a hosted flow; the tool touches ONLY private messages (DMs) — no feed, no stories, no posts.
- the subscription key lives on the **Unipile account** connector (your BYO key, or the platform's with the **hosted messaging** option granted by an admin);
- then "Connect my Instagram account" here. no cookie to paste, no extension.

## usage — read and reply from your account

`instagram_chat(op=list|read|send)` — you act as yourself, under your own account.
- "read my unread Instagram DMs"
- "reply to [handle]"
- "summarize my conversation with [handle]"

## note — message requests are not threads

DMs of a pro account and those of the "message requests" inbox are not always served together: a missing thread is often a thread awaiting acceptance on the Instagram side.
